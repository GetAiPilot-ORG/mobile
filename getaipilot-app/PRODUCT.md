# GetAIPilot mobile

GetAIPilot is an Expo React Native app for managing Telegram, WhatsApp, voice calling, social publishing, and CRM work. It also offers forms, links, page builders, and other small creation tools.

The app helps users open connected products, manage conversations and campaigns, follow activity, and handle account and subscription settings. Product availability follows existing subscriptions.

This context is inferred from the existing implementation and the user's redesign request. The explicit brief is to apply the attached pastel mobile reference across the app and remove unnecessary visual details and copy. Product workflows, billing facts, validation, and data sources remain authoritative in the implementation.
